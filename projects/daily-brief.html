AI agent · Personal project · 2026

Daily Brief

I wanted one place to read the news that matters to me each morning, without opening ten sites and a pile of newsletters. So I built an AI agent that does it for me.

  • Claude scheduled agent
  • Claude Artifacts
  • HTML / JavaScript
  • Prompt design

1What it does

Every morning a scheduled Claude agent reads my newsletters and a set of news sites, picks the most important stories of the last 24 hours, writes the summaries and publishes them to a private page. Then it sends me a short push notification with the top story.

The stories are split into seven categories:

  • Portugal Macro
  • Portugal Business
  • Geopolitics
  • Markets
  • M&A
  • AI
  • Other

2How it works

  1. CollectReads the newsletters I've labelled for it, plus about 20 news sources, from Observador and ECO to the FT, Reuters and Bloomberg.
  2. RankMerges the same story across sources and scores it on impact, novelty, durability and relevance to me. Skips anything already covered in the last 7 days.
  3. Write3 to 5 stories per category, each with a full summary, key takeaways and one line on why it matters. The Portuguese categories are written in European Portuguese.
  4. PublishPosts the brief to the page and sends a push notification with the day's hot topic.

3The page and the guardrails

What the page does

  • Today's brief by category, with the hot topic first
  • A 7-day calendar to go back to recent briefs
  • Favourites, plus 👍 / 👎 ratings the agent learns from
  • Light and dark mode

Guardrails I built in

  • Read-only email access, limited to one label. It never replies, sends or deletes anything.
  • Web pages and emails are treated as data, never as instructions, so a page can't hijack the agent.
  • Only facts found in the sources. No invented numbers, quotes or names.
  • A dry-run mode to test a brief before anything is published.
